# DM Scripts

## First DM (agents, personalized)
> Hi [Name], saw your [listing detail, e.g. "Marina penthouse listing"] on PropertyFinder. I run an AI studio that helps Dubai agents answer every inquiry in seconds, 24/7 (most lose up to 70% of leads to slow replies). Made you a quick sample of what your brand could look like. Want me to send it? Free, no strings.

## If they say "send it"
> [Sample reel concept + 1 graphic. Then:] This takes us 48 hours to produce live, from your raw phone clips. If your inquiry volume is over 20/week, the math on rescuing even 30% of them pays this back with one viewing.

## Price pitch (when they bite)
> Most agents I talk to lose leads at midnight and on weekends. Our WhatsApp AI answers every inquiry instantly, sends listing details, and books viewings straight into your calendar. Setup is 1,500 AED, then 2,500/month. One extra commission covers a full year. Want a 15-minute demo this week?

## Follow-up 1 (48h, no reply)
> [Name], quick nudge — happy to send the free sample anyway. Worst case you get a free reel for your page.

## Follow-up 2 (day 5)
> Last one from me, promise. If leads slipping through unanswered is not a problem for you, ignore this. If it is, one demo fixes it.

## Objection: "I already have an agency / I do it myself"
> Makes sense. Quick question though — who answers your Instagram DMs at 11pm? That's where Dubai buyers actually message from. That's all we'd handle, your agency keeps the rest.

## Objection: "Too expensive"
> Fair. What's your average commission? [Wait.] One rescued deal a month covers us 10-20x. If we don't book you extra viewings in 60 days, you can stop.

## Objection: "You're in India?"
> Correct — that's why it's 2,500 AED and not 15,000 like the local agencies, for the same or faster work. Everything is delivered on your accounts; you'll never notice a border.

## The close
> Cool. I'll set the bot up on a 14-day trial — you only pay setup if you keep it. Send me your WhatsApp Business number and I'll have the demo ready in 48 hours.
