# COMPETITOR RESEARCH — Oct 10, 2026
Sources checked via web search on 2026-10-10. Items marked [V] verified against a source page; [H] = hypothesis/estimate.

## GoHighLevel — the all-in-one agencies resell
- [V] Pricing $97–$497/month (review sites, 2025). Automation + CRM + funnels in one.
- [V] Common complaints in reviews: complexity/learning curve, support quality complaints.
- [H] Our wedge: GHL is generic and complex for a solo agent; we sell an installed, managed machine, not software to learn. We compete with service, not features.

## Follow Up Boss — real-estate CRM incumbent
- [V] Starts ~$69/user/month; Pro tier ~$499/mo for 10 users (comparisons, 2025-2026).
- [V] Praise: lead routing, follow-up automation, 200+ lead source integrations.
- [V] Weakness cited: no built-in website, cost grows with team size.
- [H] Agents in IN/UAE markets we target typically do NOT pay $69+/user — they run on WhatsApp + Excel. Our price point ($950–3,250/mo managed) includes labor, which CRMs do not.

## kvCORE ($499/mo) / Sierra Interactive (~$400/mo) / Ylopo / Lofty / Real Geeks / CINC
- [V] All are US-centric IDX/MLS platforms; pricing from comparison pages, 2025-2026. Their IDX/MLS model does not apply in IN/AE where portals (99acres, MagicBricks, PropertyFinder, Bayut) dominate. [H] Little direct threat in our target markets; their pattern (lead routing + automated follow-up) validates ours.

## WhatsApp BSPs (the messaging layer we will ride on)
- [V] Meta changed WhatsApp Business Platform pricing to per-template-message (effective Jul 1, 2025), then further changes from Oct 1, 2025. Cost depends on country category.
- [V] BSP tools (Interakt, WATI, respond.io, Qontak) charge roughly $55–75+/month platform fee plus Meta message costs.
- [H] Budget model: at ≤25 clients with ≤2,000 template messages/client/month, messaging cost is material and MUST be tracked per org in-app (built: usage fields exist in schema; metering = Phase 6).

## Conclusion (drives the build)
1. Differentiation is NOT another CRM UI — it is the managed install + AI follow-up discipline + niche focus.
2. Build v1: CRM + tasks + appointments + workflow engine + honest integrations + founder console. (In progress.)
3. Do NOT build IDX/MLS, website builders, funnel builders — out of scope, unnecessary in IN/AE.
