# The Daily Drill (drill-sergeant rules)

## Non-negotiables
1. 5 personalized DMs sent before lunch. Every day. No exceptions.
2. Every reply logged in the tracker with next-step and date.
3. Every sample delivered within 48 hours of a lead saying "send it".
4. Follow-ups go out on schedule (48h, day 5, then park).

## Weekly
- Monday: review tracker, chase all warm leads
- Wednesday: content delivery day for clients
- Friday: numbers review (DMs sent, replies, calls booked, deals)

## The only metric that matters this month
Conversations started with agents. 150 DMs in week 1-3 = pipeline.

## When a lead goes quiet
Nothing is dead. Park them, re-engage in 30 days with a new sample or market stat.
