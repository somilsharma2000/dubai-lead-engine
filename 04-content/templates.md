# Content Templates for Property Agents

## Reel concepts (repeatable formats)
1. "Price reveal" — slow pan, price slams on screen, "Worth it? You decide."
2. "POV: viewing a 1M AED apartment in Marina" — walkthrough with honest commentary
3. "3 areas under 1M AED in 2026" — talking head, fast cuts, text overlays
4. "What 200k AED/year rent gets you in Dubai" — comparison format
5. "Day in the life of a Dubai agent" — vlog style, builds personal brand
6. "The listing nobody wanted (then we staged it)" — before/after with AI staging
7. "5 questions to scam-check your agent" — trust content, gets shared
8. Market update — news-anchor style weekly, 60 seconds

## Hook bank (first 3 seconds)
- "Nobody is telling you this about Dubai property in 2026..."
- "This apartment costs less than a London parking space."
- "I got a client 40k AED off the asking price. Here's how."
- "If you're renting in Dubai, watch this before renewing."
- "The 70% of buyers who message at midnight are why I never sleep."

## Caption formula
Hook line + 2 honest sentences + question + CTA ("DM VIEWINGS and I'll send the details")

## Monthly calendar (per agent)
- 12 reels (3/week, mix of listings + personal brand + market tips)
- 8 carousels/guides
- Daily stories (polls, price guesses, countdowns)
- 4 YouTube long-form tours
- 12 LinkedIn posts (repurposed)

## Shot list sent to client weekly (their only job)
"Monday 2pm, golden hour if possible: 6 clips of 10 seconds each — balcony view, living room pan, kitchen detail, building entrance, street/drive-by, your face saying one line: 'This one's special, ask me why.' WhatsApp them over. 15 minutes, done."
