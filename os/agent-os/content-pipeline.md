# Agent OS — Content Pipeline (repeatable weekly cycle)

## Client's only weekly job (15 min, Monday 2pm)
Send 6 clips x 10 seconds on WhatsApp: balcony view, living room pan, kitchen detail, building entrance, street/drive-by, face saying one line. Golden hour when possible.

## My production cycle (AI agent, automatic)
- Tuesday: clips edited into 3 reels (hooks, captions, music, brand colors)
- Wednesday: 2 carousels + 8 story frames + captions in client's languages
- Thursday: scheduled to Instagram for the whole week, best-time slots
- Friday: numbers pulled for the money report

## AI prompt bank (reused for every client, tone from config sheet)
1. HOOK: "Write 5 opening lines under 10 words for a Dubai property reel about {LISTING}. Bold, no clichés, one must mention price."
2. CAPTION: "Write an Instagram caption for {AGENT_NAME}: 2 honest sentences, one question, CTA 'DM VIEWINGS'. Tone: {TONE}."
3. CAROUSEL: "Outline a 6-slide carousel: '5 things to know before buying in {AREA} in 2026'. Slide 1 = hook, slide 6 = CTA."
4. STORIES: "Give me a 5-frame story sequence for an open house: poll, price guess, countdown, tour, CTA."

## Scale math
Once prompts + templates are saved, per-client content = fill-in-the-blanks. 10 clients = same Tuesday.
